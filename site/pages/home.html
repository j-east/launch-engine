What it is

Not an analysis of your idea. A collection of concrete next actions.

Launch Engine is an AI co-pilot that produces the launch to-do list you'd kill for — specific, prioritized, time- and cost-estimated, and laid across your next two weeks. It reads what already exists only to figure out which steps remain, then prunes hard to the few that matter for your budget.

How it works

Six stages, from intake to launch

01

Intake & scope

What you're building, your goal, where you are now, and your time and money budget.

02

Investment options

Lean, balanced, or aggressive scenarios, with time and money sliders you control.

03

Mind map

Your idea, broken into areas and concrete steps, clustered into build and market work.

04

Schedule

A day-by-day 14-day agenda with a budget across every activity.

05

Daily check-ins

Log wins and progress; the plan re-sequences to stay honest.

06

Final review

What shipped, what you avoided, and where the budget actually went.

The idea behind it

Left brain builds. Right brain markets.

Launch Engine organizes the work around one intuitive picture: a brain with two hemispheres. It's a map of the work, not a verdict on you.

Left · the build

Product, QA, ops, scheduling, accounting, legal, infra. Structured and sequential — the builder's comfort zone.

Right · the market

Positioning, content, audience, distribution, PR. Generative and relational — usually the founder's hardest side.
