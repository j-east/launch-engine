How it works

From product and budget to a scheduled launch plan

Launch Engine runs your product through six stages. Each one turns what you know into concrete, prioritized actions, then fits them to your real time and money budget across the next two weeks.

The six stages

Intake to launch

01

Intake & scope

A light read of what already exists, purely to scope which steps remain. No grading.

02

Investment options & key decisions

A few time and money scenarios; you adjust the sliders and make the trade-offs.

03

Mind map

Central idea to areas to steps, each with time and cost estimates, priority, and a scoped chat to refine them.

04

Schedule

The optimizer's allocation turned into a concrete 14-day agenda.

05

Daily check-ins

Log wins and improvement areas; the plan re-sequences what's left.

06

Final review & report

A retrospective on what shipped, what got avoided, and the launch outcome.
