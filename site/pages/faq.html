Common questions

The basics

What is Launch Engine?

An AI co-pilot that turns your product, goal, timeline, and budget into a prioritized, scheduled launch to-do list — and keeps it current as you work. It generates actions, it doesn't grade your idea.

Who is it for?

Solo founders, indie hackers, and first-time builders shipping a product on a tight time and money budget.

How is it different from a generic planner?

Generic planners are empty boxes you fill with your own bias. Launch Engine generates the right actions for your specific product and budget, then ruthlessly prioritizes and sequences them.

Does it build the product for me?

No. It tells you what to do next and in what order. The building — and the marketing — is still yours.

How long is the plan?

It's laid across a 14-day schedule by default, fit to the time and money you have.

Digital products only?

The taxonomy is general, but the current path is tuned for digital products first.
