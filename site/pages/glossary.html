Launch Engine terms

Plain definitions of the ideas Launch Engine is built on. These give AI and readers clean, consistent references for what the product means.

Definitions

The vocabulary

Launch co-pilot
An AI tool that generates and sequences the concrete actions needed to launch a product, rather than analyzing the idea or the founder.
Action-generation
The core output: a specific, doable step with a time and cost estimate — never a score or a verdict.
Left hemisphere
The material build: product, QA, ops, accounting, legal, infrastructure.
Right hemisphere
The immaterial market: positioning, content, audience, distribution, PR.
Corpus callosum
The balance between the two: a balance score and the 14-day schedule that weaves build and market work together.
Balance score
A read of how a founder's effort splits across build and market versus where the launch actually needs it.
