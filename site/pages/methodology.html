Methodology

The left-brain / right-brain map

Launch Engine organizes launch work around a brain with two hemispheres: the left is the material build, the right is the immaterial market, and the corpus callosum in the middle is the balance — a score and a 14-day schedule that weave both sides together.

Two hemispheres

Where every action belongs

Left · material build

Product and development, QA, operations, scheduling, accounting, legal and entity, infrastructure. Structured, sequential, logical — the builder's comfort zone.

Right · immaterial market

Idea and validation, positioning, content and creative, audience and network, distribution, PR. Generative, holistic, relational — usually the founder's hardest side, so we model it richest.

Two honest rules

A metaphor, not neuroscience

Left-brain / right-brain is pop psychology. We use it for instant, intuitive structure — a way to see at a glance how a launch splits across build and market — and never claim it's clinical. The binary also carries gradients: the right hemisphere produces material artifacts (videos, ad creative, posts), the left makes immaterial decisions (UX, architecture).
